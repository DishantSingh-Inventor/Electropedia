Communication Bus Protocols

Explore in-depth timings, line topologies and byte frame anatomies for essential embedded communications.

  • I2C (Inter-Integrated Circuit) - 2-wire synchronous bus with open-drain pull-ups.
  • SPI (Serial Peripheral Interface) - 4-wire high-speed synchronous full-duplex protocol.
  • UART (Asynchronous Serial) - Point-to-point cross-connected TX/RX transmission
  • CAN Bus 2.0 & CAN-FD - Differential automotive bus with collision arbitration.
  • 1-Wire (Dallas / Maxim) - Single data line protocol with parasitic power.
